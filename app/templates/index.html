Fake product review detection

Evidence first.

ReviewTrust combines three AI stages to assess a product review. Each stage passes its evidence forward, turning a prediction into a result you can inspect and understand.

Start an analysis ↓
  1. 01Classify

    A calibrated language model predicts whether the review is deceptive and checks whether its confidence is high enough to accept.

  2. 02Extract

    The entity model highlights the exact words and phrases that may contain suspicious claims, incentives, or manipulation.

  3. 03Explain

    A Qwen reasoner uses both outputs as private evidence and returns a concise, readable assessment.
